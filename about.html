My Story

From drawing UI templates to building a real product from zero.

Vibe coding & design: How I got started

During my gap year before BBS, I didn't want to just sit around. I decided to learn design and building by actually doing it. I spent months learning Figma, visual design, UX principles, and connecting with actual designers and builders in Nepal's startup space.

This hands-on journey led me to KHOZNA. When students move to Kathmandu for college, finding a place to stay is a headache. Listings are outdated, photos are fake, and landlords have no professional way to showcase their properties. It's a mess.

Instead of just wireframing a mockup, I wanted to build the whole solution. I designed the UX/UI end-to-end, ran user research with actual renters and owners, and then vibe coded the full-stack app with the help of AI assistants.
