My Story

From drawing UI templates to building a real product from zero — this is how I got here.

From curious learner to product builder

During my gap year before BBS, I made a deliberate choice — instead of waiting, I would learn by doing. I spent months mastering Figma, studying UX fundamentals, completing case studies, and building real connections within Nepal's design and startup community.

That curiosity led to KHOZNA. I saw how students moving to Kathmandu for college struggled to find trusted, verified rental listings. Landlords had no professional way to present their properties. Renters wasted weeks visiting physical sites for listings that often turned out to be wrong or unavailable.

I decided to design the solution myself — leading end-to-end UX/UI, running user research with real renters and landlords, and eventually building a full-stack web application with AI-assisted tools. What started as a design project became a business.

I believe design is a decision-making process, not a decoration exercise. Every pixel, every flow, every word on a screen is either helping a user or hurting them. My job is to make sure it helps.
